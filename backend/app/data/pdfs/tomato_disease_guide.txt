crop: Tomato
disease: Late Blight
symptoms: Dark water-soaked lesions on leaves and stems; white mould on leaf undersides in humid conditions; rapid collapse of foliage.
treatment: Remove and destroy infected plant parts; apply copper-based or mancozeb fungicide every 7–10 days; avoid overhead irrigation.

crop: Tomato
disease: Early Blight
symptoms: Brown spots with concentric rings (target-board pattern) on older leaves; yellow halo around spots; defoliation from bottom up.
treatment: Apply chlorothalonil or mancozeb fungicide; remove infected lower leaves; practice crop rotation.

crop: Tomato
disease: Septoria Leaf Spot
symptoms: Small circular spots with dark borders and grey-white centres; spots appear first on lower leaves; heavy spotting causes leaf drop.
treatment: Apply fungicide at first sign; remove infected leaves; avoid wetting foliage; rotate crops.

crop: Tomato
disease: Fusarium Wilt
symptoms: Yellowing and wilting of lower leaves; brown discolouration inside the stem; plants die progressively.
treatment: Use resistant varieties; remove and destroy infected plants; practice long crop rotations; avoid over-watering.

crop: Tomato
disease: Bacterial Speck
symptoms: Small dark-brown to black spots surrounded by yellow halo on leaves; raised scab-like spots on fruit.
treatment: Use certified disease-free seed; apply copper-based bactericide; avoid working in field when wet.

crop: Tomato
disease: Tomato Mosaic Virus
symptoms: Mosaic pattern of light and dark green on leaves; leaf distortion and stunting; reduced fruit set.
treatment: No chemical cure; remove infected plants; control aphid vectors; use virus-resistant varieties.

crop: Tomato
disease: Powdery Mildew
symptoms: White powdery coating on upper leaf surface; leaves curl upward; premature defoliation.
treatment: Apply sulphur-based fungicide or potassium bicarbonate; improve air circulation; avoid excess nitrogen.

crop: Tomato
disease: Blossom End Rot
symptoms: Dark, sunken, leathery patch at the blossom end of fruit; not caused by a pathogen.
treatment: Maintain consistent soil moisture; apply calcium foliar spray; avoid excess nitrogen and root damage.
