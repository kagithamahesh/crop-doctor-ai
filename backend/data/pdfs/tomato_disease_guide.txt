crop: Tomato
disease: Late Blight
symptoms: Dark water-soaked lesions on leaves and stems. White fungal growth under humid conditions.
treatment: Remove infected leaves. Apply Mancozeb or Chlorothalonil fungicide. Improve field drainage.

crop: Tomato
disease: Early Blight
symptoms: Brown concentric ring spots on lower leaves.
treatment: Spray copper fungicide or Mancozeb. Remove infected plant debris.

crop: Tomato
disease: Leaf Mold
symptoms: Yellow spots on upper leaf surface and olive-green mold underneath.
treatment: Improve ventilation and apply appropriate fungicide.
